Dice War

Printable rulebook

About the game

Dice War is a two-player strategy game about moving your dice into your opponent's zone while removing their dice before they reach yours. A game takes about 30 minutes and uses ten six-sided dice—five of each color. A twenty-sided die can be used for scorekeeping, but is optional.
